Privacy

Last updated August 12, 2026

01 / The short version

This portfolio does not run analytics, advertising, or a contact-form backend.

You can browse the site without creating an account or sending personal information to this website.

02 / Local preferences

Theme choice

The light or dark theme you select is saved in your browser’s local storage under jl-theme. It stays on your device and can be removed by clearing this site’s stored data.

03 / Direct contact

Email

Email links open your own email application. Any message you choose to send is handled by your email provider and the recipient’s email provider, not by this website.

04 / External services

Outbound links

Project, GitHub, and LinkedIn links lead to third-party sites with their own privacy practices. The 3D object renders locally in your browser and does not upload device or interaction data.
