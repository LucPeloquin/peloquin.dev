## ThreeUI Community source techniques

The Selected Work WebGL visual substantially adapts procedural techniques from the following ThreeUI Community examples at pinned commit [`fbc9b3d61b0ef4b2e93b42e4fffa617ca277429b`](https://github.com/MengTo/threeui/commit/fbc9b3d61b0ef4b2e93b42e4fffa617ca277429b):

- [`platform-core.html`](https://github.com/MengTo/threeui/blob/fbc9b3d61b0ef4b2e93b42e4fffa617ca277429b/src/shaders/neuform-isolated/sources/platform-core.html): instanced platform and core composition.
- [`nexus-topology.html`](https://github.com/MengTo/threeui/blob/fbc9b3d61b0ef4b2e93b42e4fffa617ca277429b/src/shaders/neuform-isolated/sources/nexus-topology.html): deterministic point-and-connection topology field.
- [`vertex-9.html`](https://github.com/MengTo/threeui/blob/fbc9b3d61b0ef4b2e93b42e4fffa617ca277429b/src/shaders/neuform-isolated/sources/vertex-9.html): deformable parallel-line data surface.
